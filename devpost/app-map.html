TermLens: app map

A take-home guide to what you built: follow one click from your text to the explanation card.

The path of one paragraph

Three places in the code

  1. 1. Finding terms: text.mjs → findTerms(). Tries 3- and 2-word phrases first, then single words in their dictionary form (lemmaCandidates()). A word counts as difficult when its frequency score (zipf) is below the slider value.
  2. 2. Choosing the meaning: text.mjs → rankSenses(). This is the simplified Lesk algorithm: every meaning gets one point per word its definition and example share with the sentences around the term. posHint() adds a hint from the word form ("diffuses" → verb or noun).
  3. 3. Showing it: app.js → showCard(). Takes the top-ranked meaning and builds the card. "Add to glossary" calls glossary.mjs → add(), which saves it in your browser.
Try a safe edit (optional)

In index.html, change value="3.6" on the #level slider to value="3.0". Reload: fewer words are highlighted at the start. Change it back afterwards, or keep it if you prefer.

Why Lesk is a good first step (and its limits)

It needs no training data and is fully explainable: the card even shows which words matched. Its limit is short definitions with few shared words. A modern next step would be comparing sentence embeddings, which is a nice small ML project for later.
