# TermLens

Paste a paragraph from your course material and the words that block you light up. One click explains each in plain language, with an example, and keeps it in your personal glossary.

## The Unique Kernel
It explains exactly the terms *you* stumble over, in the context of *your* text, and turns them into your own glossary while you read. It is not a generic dictionary search and not a rewrite of the whole text.

## Who It's For
Any university student reading dense course material (lecture notes, scripts, textbook pages), especially first-year students and students studying in a language that is not their first. For the competition: English material. Today they stop, open a dictionary or search engine in another tab, lose their place, and forget the term a week later.

## The Core Loop
Open TermLens → paste a paragraph → difficult terms are highlighted → click a term → plain explanation + example + simpler word → it is added to the glossary → continue reading. They come back with the next paragraph, and the glossary grows into revision flashcards.

## Inspiration & Identity
Calm, focused reading tool. Not flashy. Should feel like a helpful study buddy, not a test.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"einfach es funktioniert und hilft menschen": it should simply work and help people understand more easily.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In the demo, a student pastes a paragraph from a biology or physics text. About ten hard terms are highlighted. They click "osmosis" and see a short explanation, an example sentence and simpler words. A "Simplify" view shows the paragraph with simpler words, and a readability score shows it got easier. The glossary lists the looked-up terms and exports as flashcards.

## The POC Boundary
- Paste text (English).
- Detect difficult terms in the text.
- Explanation card per term: definition matching the context, example, simpler synonyms.
- "Simplify" view with a readability score before/after.
- Personal glossary in the browser, export as flashcards (CSV for Anki/Quizlet).

## Later
- PDF and slide upload.
- Other languages (German first).
- AI-written everyday examples and explanations tailored to a subject.
- Quiz mode from the glossary.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts and cloud sync: not needed to prove the kernel, and they add privacy issues.
- Rewriting whole texts with AI: different product (option B), and it needs an AI service.
